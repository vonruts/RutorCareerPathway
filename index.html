01 / CAREER PATHWAY

From support
to systems thinking.

A progression from responding to individual needs to shaping the infrastructure that supports entire environments.

FOUNDATION
ENGINEERING

First point of contact for users and technology issues. Build practical diagnostic habits while learning how devices and services work together.

RESPONSIBILITIES
  • Resolve hardware, software, and access issues.
  • Prepare devices and manage support tickets.
  • Document fixes and escalate complex incidents.
SKILLS → NEXT MILESTONE
  • OS, networking, and user support fundamentals.
  • Practice account and permission management.
  • Progress toward junior system administration.

Maintain the health, access, and continuity of server environments and shared services.

RESPONSIBILITIES
  • Manage servers, accounts, updates, and permissions.
  • Monitor services, backups, storage, and performance.
  • Investigate incidents and maintain recovery procedures.
SKILLS → NEXT MILESTONE
  • Windows Server, Linux, identity, and virtualization concepts.
  • PowerShell or Bash for repeatable tasks.
  • Progress toward senior administration or engineering.

Plan resilient infrastructure, improve operations through automation, and guide technical decisions across systems.

RESPONSIBILITIES
  • Design and improve server and infrastructure architecture.
  • Lead incident reviews and resilience planning.
  • Establish practical standards for security and monitoring.
SKILLS → NEXT MILESTONE
  • Advanced networking, cloud, and infrastructure planning.
  • Automation and clear technical documentation.
  • Continue toward infrastructure leadership and specialization.

Career titles and requirements vary by organization. Progress depends on experience, projects, and continuous learning.
